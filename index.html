      Field notes

      • You can absorb any body 20% lighter than you. Green names are prey, red names are predators.
      • The solid red ring around a predator is its point of no return. Inside it, only a boost gets you out.
      • Gravity surge doubles your pull for a few seconds. It recharges in 14 seconds.
      • Nebulae hide you from hunters but slow you down.
      • Wormholes take bodies up to 400 mass across the system.
      • A supernova blasts everything nearby, then leaves stardust and a pulsar worth 45.
